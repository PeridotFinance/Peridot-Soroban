# Medium 2: exact receipt rounding and explicit permanent seed

A capture is reproduced using public borrow/interest/repay operations. Direct SAC donations do not produce the reported capture because receipt NAV uses tracked `ManagedCash`, not the raw token balance. The defect is the second rounding step when financial mint/payout calculations use the already truncated exchange-rate quote.

Let `A` be supplier NAV, `S` total raw pTokens and `D` actually received underlying. Previously `r=floor(A*10^6/S)`, mint `floor(D*10^6/r)`, and non-final payout `floor(burn*r/10^6)`. The final holder already received exact remaining supplier NAV and unwound all strategy shares. NAV is managed cash plus strategy value plus debt minus reserves and admin fees; direct donations remain outside it.

The scoped-auth native reproduction deposits three raw units, borrows one at a TEST-only 100% annual static rate, advances one artificial year, and repays two. No economic storage value is injected. NAV becomes four with three shares and the quote is 1,333,333. A new depositor supplies 1,333,333 raw units and receives 1,000,000 shares, although the exact mint is only 999,999. The incumbent's three-share redemption pays three rather than its four-unit entitlement; the entrant then exits last for 1,333,334, capturing one raw unit. This is an arithmetic proof, not a live opportunity, fee-adjusted profitable strategy, or time/archival qualification.

The pre-fix evidence is `target/medium2-rounding-20261111/pre-fix-native.log` and the preserved `pre-fix-rounding-regression-test.rs`. Both pre-fix scenarios passed their explicit reproduction assertions. The separate original direct-donation test covers a one-share first deposit, donation of 999, subsequent deposit of 1,500, both last-holder orders, zero-supply reset and the next front-running one-unit depositor. No party captures the 999 donated units; they remain physically present but outside NAV.

The implementation now uses exact U256 products and floor division: mint `floor(D*S/A)` and payout `floor(burn*A/S)`. The 1e6 quote remains available for informational and conservative collateral calculations; there is no amount-out withdrawal ABI and no new claim about ceil-burning shares. A single pre-transfer NAV snapshot prices mint; a single withdrawal NAV snapshot supplies payout and its health quote. U256 avoids intermediate u128 product overflow and rejects an unrepresentable u128 result.

The intermediate fixed reproduction minted 999,999, paid the incumbent four and the entrant its 1,333,333 contribution. That compatibility test is preserved as historical evidence. The final release no longer selects legacy behavior from absent policy. Its current financial regression uses the real 1,000-share seed: earned interest produces NAV 1,004 / supply 1,003; a 100,000,000-unit entrant receives exact-floor 99,900,398 shares rather than rounded-quote 99,900,399. Both payouts match the exact current NAV ratio, no entrant profit is created, and permanent supply remains 1,000.

## Explicit permanent funding

`seed_minimum_supply(admin, seed_assets) -> u128` is a separate authenticated operation. It requires the configured cash policy, no CL custody mode or boosted strategy, zero NAV/supply/debt/managed cash and no reserve/admin claims. Seed assets are actually transferred from the stored admin and must be received exactly. The stored supply cap is mandatory and applies to seed funding. The initial rate is mandatory initialized state. Wrong admin, missing auth, seed below the minimum, cap excess and repeated seeding reject.

All seed shares are minted to the receipt's own address. `PermanentMinimumSupply` is an appended INSTANCE key, immutable through the current ABI. `get_minimum_supply()` reports it. The minimum is **1,000 raw pToken units**; the tested initial rate is 1:1, so funding costs 1,000 raw underlying units (0.0001000 units for a seven-decimal SAC). It is not 1,000 whole tokens, a sufficient-economic-seed recommendation, or a guarantee that the locked claim will preserve principal. The operator must select and separately approve the actual seed allocation. This document leaves that amount blank. Any ordinary withdrawable operator deposit is a separate transaction/allocation.

Ordinary configured cash deposits reject until the explicit floor is funded; no ordinary depositor silently supplies the floor. The permanent allocation remains in total share supply and NAV. It cannot be withdrawn, transferred (including transfer_from), or seized below the floor. Withdrawal also enforces the aggregate floor. There is no admin unlock/reset selector. CL custody does not take this cash-only seed path and retains its separate 1:1 requirement. In the final default cash release, missing reserve configuration means a 100% reserve, never legacy routing; borrowing remains false. The separate lp-engine feature retains its existing optional policy. No old deployment is upgraded or migrated.

The native seed test pays 1,000 from the admin, mints zero shares to the admin and 1,000 to the receipt, then shows an ordinary 1,234-unit deposit creates all 1,234 shares for its depositor and fully redeems. The next seven-unit roundtrip leaves the floor and supplier NAV at 1,000. A pre-seed raw donation of 99 does not affect the 1:1 seed quote or NAV. A synthetic seizure-context issuer uses actual contract-invoker authorization: it fails to take one locked share with complete ledger/TTL rollback, while the same nested call can seize one ordinary holder share. This isolates the floor enforcement and is not a controller liquidation-pricing proof. Transfer and withdrawal reuse the same floor helper; no test mocks the receipt's own authority to manufacture an impossible self-signature.
