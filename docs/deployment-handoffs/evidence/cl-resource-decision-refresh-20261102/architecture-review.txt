Scope: existing handoffs 12 and 15 and their cited measurements. No tests,

RECOMMENDED NEXT PRIORITY

Keep the existing atomic ownership/custody transitions as the reference
architecture. First determine whether the unchanged baseline can satisfy a
separately approved bounded policy with credible signed/restored/stressed
headroom. Do not promote the split prototype or implement a policy increase.
The useful next engineering work is tightly measured removal of redundant
within-invocation configuration/serialization work and reduction of genuinely
touched receipt/controller keys, preserving every claim-first boundary.

If the policy decision remains 100M/100 SDK entries/50 writes, make the next
feasibility milestone a venue/accounting ABI experiment, not further division
of the product into transactions. The measured unchanged venue work already
exceeds 100M in deposit/redeem and margin open/close. A compact/fused venue
operation and narrower receipt key access need proof and benchmarks before
promising this target. These are alternatives for an explicit decision, not
permission to apply either one now.

WHY THE ATOMIC BASELINE DESERVES FIRST EVALUATION

Handoff12's separate proposal is 300M CPU, 300 SDK entry total, 300 unique
transaction keys, 100 writes and 32MiB modeled memory. Its proposed 20% workload
reserve means supported worst-case consumption must be <=240M CPU, <=240 SDK
entries, <=240 unique keys, <=80 writes and <=26,843,545 bytes. This is a
reserve inside the proposed policy, distinct from unused network capacity.

These are independent maxima across controlled executions and two builds,
not quantities from one transaction; neither memory nor key maxima should be
added together across populations. CPU room is only about 1.38% of the 240M
workload boundary. Fitting an unsigned sample does not establish headroom for
real account authorization, larger history/tick crossproducts or new patches.
The memory proposal is tighter than today's 40MiB policy, not a blanket
relaxation. A direct switch to captured-network diagnostics is not Option B.

The baseline architecture retains the established atomic share acquisition
and custody transitions and avoids the prototype's new debt-bearing escrow
states. This is a smaller correctness change than adopting staged custody.
It still requires all baseline recovery/reserve/authorization proofs; resource
fit never cures an accounting or liquidation defect.

Retain every other byte/event/code/fee/rent/restoration check from handoff12.
SDK read + write + disk-read totals are a separate diagnostic from the union
of transaction LedgerKeys. A lower unique-key count does not excuse an SDK
entry failure, and fewer repeated getters need not reduce first-touch keys.

WHAT RETAINING 100M ACTUALLY REQUIRES

Memo12 full SDK CPU: deposit166.915M, redeem139.752M, swap-open235.457M,
swap-close184.220M. Reaching 100M requires approximately 40.1%, 28.4%, 57.5%
and 45.7% reductions respectively, before additional safety headroom. The
corresponding unchanged disjoint venue-subtree sums are134.355M,126.766M,
147.936M and130.983M. They are floors conditional on keeping those calls,
not proof that each individual call or every redesigned transaction exceeds
100M. Handoff15 correctly demonstrates a ~63.7M Aquarius deposit subtree.

Concrete tightly scoped work:

1. Benchmark a reviewed venue primitive that combines fee realization with
   position mutation, avoiding repeated position/tick/fee-growth traversal.
   Current collection (~52.9M) plus withdrawal (~62.2M) already consume more
   than100M before reward and surrounding logic. Share pricing must account
   for incumbent fees before mint/burn. Prove partial-owner allocation,
   fractional carry, range crossings, actual receipts and slippage. This
   requires new venue ABI/bytecode if the pinned ABI cannot support it;
   presumed savings are zero until measured with actual bytecode.
2. Preserve actual reward claims at deposit, share transfer and custody
   assignment. Optimize repeated internal work within each boundary only.
   Fresh valuation/authorization dependencies after mutations cannot be
   replaced with stale pre-transaction values. History cannot be pruned or
   quarantined value reassigned to simplify accounting.
3. Reduce touched controller/receipt/custody keys with verified minimal read
   sets or versioned compact configuration. Measure packing's migration,
   write amplification, rent, entry size and restoration costs. Funded
   liquidation213 SDK entries/56 writes, activation182 entries, receipt
   ownership124 entries and close preparation120 entries are independent
   problems; CPU-only venue work cannot solve them.

Wrapper-only cleanup is not a credible route to the large deficits. Conversely,
small proven savings may matter to Option B's narrow remaining CPU room, but
cannot be credited without a same-state, same-runtime comparison and complete
transaction evidence. Unmeasured signature costs cannot be declared smaller
than the available3.305M.

STAGED ARCHITECTURE ADMISSION — ALL CONDITIONS REQUIRED

The current split is a negative feasibility experiment, not the next default.
Its successful conversion/preparation calls do not establish a usable whole
product. Mint131.9M (136M with another owner), activation181 entries,
prepare-close120 entries, swap-close~191.5M unsigned CPU/173 SDK entries and
late minted/delivered recoveries127/123 entries remain failures under current
policy. Begin at100 entries has zero measured entry headroom. The supplemental
recovery results supersede any inference that source-level recovery availability
means policy-executable recovery.

Before any future staged adoption, require:

* Every required complete root fits the selected policy and approved headroom:
  fund, conversion, preparation, mint, delivery, activation, ordinary direct
  deposit/redeem, close, each cancellation/retry, every phase's repayment and
  funded liquidation, including rejection/rollback, restoration and supported
  authorization. Captured setup of a later stage is not a passing prefix.
* Every intermediate state with outstanding debt has borrower-independent
  recovery/liquidation. A third party must be able to fund repayment and seize
  only independently valued permitted collateral, returning surplus, atomically
  with debt reduction. Actual interest, custody, rewards, deadline and replay
  state must be included. Owner external cash or owner signature cannot be the
  only safety route. No governance attribution, active keeper or waiver may be
  required for a forced exit. No debt forgiveness or expiry-based seizure.
* Progress remains feasible while permissionless pool trades continue. The
  present exact fee/state fence safely rejects stale work but can be invalidated
  repeatedly between preparation and mint. Same-deadline retry is bounded
  cancellation behavior, not evidence of progress. A candidate needs a fresh
  fee-inclusive final valuation/settlement primitive that tolerates intervening
  admissible trades, or another proved design with a bounded in-policy fallback
  that can complete safely. It cannot depend on locking the public pool, finding
  a quiet ledger, a cooperative borrower or unbounded re-preparation fees.
* Preserve beneficial ownership from mint through escrow, physical delivery and
  margin custody; all three original actual reward boundaries remain. Preserve
  exited beneficiaries and unresolved intervals under outages. Demonstrate
  fractional LP fee carry, ownership/emission changes, stale evidence, donated
  assets and overlapping jobs without newcomer windfalls or shared-wallet sweeps.
* Define and test phase-specific pauses, expiry, cancellation and recovery.
  Current late delivery/activation/permissionless expired resolution must be
  described accurately. Risk limits cannot silently block repayment or forced
  recovery, and ordinary progress cannot silently bypass final reserve checks.
* Fund and measure the new escrow's archival/restoration and persistent history
  obligations. Baseline restoration costs do not prove new-state costs. Preserve
  all unresolved entitlements; admission bounds must stop new risk before they
  make existing debt/reward recovery inoperable.

A design with no debt until a final atomic transition could avoid some new
liquidation states, but it has not been implemented or measured and must still
fit final mint, activation, close and recovery. It is not a claimed solution.

EVIDENCE LIMITS / RECONCILIATIONS

Handoff12's numeric proposal uses ac63255 build populations. Handoff15 compares
the later9cf4308 baseline and a larger prototype binary; the reserve/refund
patch and additional tests must not inherit older cost approval. Refreshing
the existing archive analysis is appropriate, not a reason to rerun or change
source within this advice task. Raw reports for each build stay separate.

The apparent147.9M versus63.7M conflict is a sum of multiple venue calls versus
one deposit subtree, not contradictory measurements. Direct exit measures in
the prototype are not byte-identical baseline A/B proofs. A later phase fitting
captured limits is not an internal-policy lifecycle pass. Handoff15's23-case
combined outcome comprises the main21 tests plus a separately preserved two-
case recovery supplement; the original main log must not be relabelled.

Real signed authorization/serialization, combined worst-case history/tick/range/
restoration workloads, supported asset/oracle conditions and live-state evidence
remain gates. No-signing restrictions remain intact; unavailable evidence stays
missing. The verified network capture is pinned historical state, not a guarantee
that its ceilings or pricing remain suitable at future deployment.

Decision: prioritize atomic-baseline resource reconciliation and bounded
optimization while explicitly resolving the policy question. If100M remains
mandatory, require venue/ABI and touched-key feasibility evidence before more
staging work. Do not adopt the present split merely because early stages fit,
and do not approve300M merely because current unsigned samples fit240M.
