# Larger historical accounting assessment

This is controlled state on the pinned Aquarius WASM and official protocol 29 runtime. It does not establish production live-state bounds or validate account signatures. The claim-first boundaries and transaction stages remain unchanged.

The 64-owner test constructs actual historical state through compiled deposit/transfer calls: 63 initial distributions plus 128 additional ownership changes. Setup calls retain invocation limits and real nested contract authorization, but setup does not claim full-transaction evidence. All subsequent assessed emergency exits, claims, segment preparation, settlements and repeated settlements use the normal instrumented helpers. Assertion and index-discovery calls execute on disposable ledger clones because query-looking APIs may settle rewards or extend TTLs. Their mutations cannot warm or alter assessed state; they enforce the same invocation limits and are not transaction evidence. Client-side checkpoint discovery uses binary search, while settlement itself reads the supplied adjacent checkpoint pair and does not enumerate holders.

Eight claim outages cause eight full beneficiary exits. The first interval is settled for all 64 historical owners after those exits; later intervals are incrementally settled for the corresponding exited owner. Remaining balances are reserved historical backing, not missing-boundary quarantine, and the exact scaled conservation equation includes them.

The second test constructs eight actual margin positions and forces eight funded exits while both the pinned pool's claim and pending view fail through a controlled malformed User record. The fault preserves the bytecode, and the record is restored after each atomic funded repayment/seizure. Sixteen missing boundaries remain missing even after later successful claims. Previously booked borrower rewards survive every forced exit. No liquidation accepts a waiver.

## Eventual attribution

Automatic settlement requires trustworthy readings at both interval boundaries, immutable complete historical beneficial weights including receipt/margin custody, the actual received claim delta, and the original waiver ledger. A later cumulative read cannot recover an earlier absent boundary. The test does not mark missing readings as automatically verified.

A governed proposal commits to a published evidence digest, a segment ID and an amount. After the 86,400-second delay, authorized attribution marks the segment explicitly governed. It does not retroactively set observed_verified. All segments in an interval must be attributed before proportional reconciliation can settle. Historical weights determine original owners, voluntary waivers determine only the eligible redistribution, and original, redistributed, later and quarantined credits remain distinct. Settlement is incremental and idempotent.

A defensible production evidence package needs the exact pool/code hash, ledger and transaction ordering for each boundary, authenticated venue accounting/state sufficient to reconstruct reward growth and tick/range participation at that boundary, emission/config changes, original vault and receipt/margin custody ownership revisions, share supply/unassigned custody, voluntary-waiver records, actual claim receipts/token deltas and rounding treatment, a reconciliation showing no double attribution, and an auditable mapping from each proposed segment amount to those records. Governance must publish the actual package, not merely a digest. The contract verifies administrator authorization, digest agreement, timing and ledger accounting; it does not determine that an arbitrary submitted evidence document is truthful.

For the first of the eight fault intervals, this fixture publishes explicitly governed controlled-replay evidence: actual pending values immediately before and after the fault at the same synthetic timestamp, no liquidity mutation during receipt seizure, and the matching actual subsequent claim. That makes all nonzero accrual precede the first ownership change in this controlled replay. This evidence is not a live missing-boundary observation, and is never labeled automatic. The original borrower receives it after exiting all custody; the newcomer receives zero for that interval.

For the other seven intervals no sufficient governed evidence is submitted. They remain quarantined, excluded from NAV and the ordinary reward accumulator, and attempted settlement must reject without changing ledger state. If authentic boundary accounting cannot eventually be produced, those amounts remain quarantined indefinitely. A current-owner list, elapsed holding time, a subsequent cumulative read or an administrator's assertion alone does not justify redistribution.

## Archive assessment

The archive rerun uses CL_TRANSACTION_ARCHIVE_ROLE=vault. The unsigned target-runtime simulator expires actual persistent vault entries in a cloned pre-invocation state, restores touched entries, charges restoration/rent, and compares result and effective poststate with the nonarchived execution. This measures the actual history keys reached by each assessed operation; it does not charge for restoring every unrelated historical key and does not claim complete network archival/live-state coverage. Persistent historical keys currently grow without pruning; retention/indexing/archival operational bounds remain an explicit limitation.
